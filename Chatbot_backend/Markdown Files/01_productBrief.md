# Product brief

## Problem
- Procurement officers and students struggle to find which rules and forms apply.
- Guidelines and Manual are long and hard to search manually.

## Users
- Procurement officers in public sector
- Students and researchers learning procurement rules

## Core features
- Ask questions in natural language
- Get answers grounded in Guidelines 2006 and Manual 2006
- Show citations for every answer

## Non functional requirements
- Response time under 3 seconds for normal questions
- All answers must include at least one citation
- System must run locally and be containerised

## Success criteria
- At least 80 percent of test questions retrieve the correct section in top 5 chunks
- Usable by a non technical person with no documentation
